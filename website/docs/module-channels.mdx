# Channels

Channels connects messaging platforms and mailbox flows to the workbench and routes inbound work to a selected reply agent.

## Open Channels when

- Suora needs to receive work from outside the desktop UI
- a reply agent should serve a platform-specific audience
- you need webhook, stream, or email-based routing

## Current surface

- webhook and stream transport modes
- reply-agent binding and allowlists
- message, user, health, and debug panels
- platform-specific support for WeChat, Feishu, DingTalk, Slack, Telegram, Discord, Teams, Email, and Custom

## Works closely with

- Agents for reply behavior
- Models for downstream AI execution
- Settings and Logs for operational safety and diagnosis
